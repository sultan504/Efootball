How it works

  1. Register your team name, phone number and your best playing time.
  2. You'll receive a one-time secret code — save it, you'll need it to submit results.
  3. The admin schedules your fixtures once the bracket opens.
  4. After a match, either player submits the score using their code.
  5. The admin verifies and approves — the bracket updates automatically.
